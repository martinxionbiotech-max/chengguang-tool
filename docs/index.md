## :material-help-circle: FAQ

### Are these tools free?
Yes. All tools on this site are **completely free** to use. No registration required.

### Do I need to download anything?
No. All tools run in your browser. No app, no download, no installation.

### How accurate are the calculators?
Calculators use established industry formulas and Chengguang's own battery specifications. Results are estimates and should be verified against your vehicle's owner manual and professional testing.

### What if I need a battery not listed here?
Chengguang manufactures 14 models across JIS and DIN standards. If your required specification is not shown, [contact us for custom OEM manufacturing](https://oem.chengguangenergy.com/).

### Can I use these tools for commercial purposes?
Yes. Distributors, mechanics, and fleet managers are welcome to use these tools. For B2B volume inquiries, [visit our OEM site](https://oem.chengguangenergy.com/).

### How do I get a quote for bulk battery orders?
Use the [Battery Finder](finder/index.md) to identify your model, then [request a quote](https://chengguangenergy.com/contact/) with the model number and your target volume.
