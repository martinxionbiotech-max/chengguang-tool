# Car Battery Buying Guide 2026

## The 5-Step Battery Selection Process

### Step 1: Determine Your Battery Standard

Your vehicle is designed for one of these standards:

| Standard | Terminal Style | Hold-down | Common Markets |
|----------|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| **JIS** | T1 flat tab (small or large) | B00 top frame | Asia, Africa, Middle East, Oceania |
| **DIN** | Round post | B13 bottom rail lip | Europe, North/West Africa |
| **BCI** | SAE post or side terminal | Various (Group-dependent) | North America |
| **AS** | JIS terminal, Australian spec | B00 | Australia |
| **SA** | JIS terminal, SA spec | B00 | South Africa |

!!! warning "JIS and DIN are NOT interchangeable"
    Different terminal types, case dimensions, and hold-down mechanisms. Using the wrong standard requires adapter kits or modification.

## FAQ

### How do I know what battery fits my car?
Check your owner's manual, look at the label on your current battery, or use our **[Battery Finder](../finder/index.md)**.

### Can I replace a JIS battery with a DIN battery?
Not directly. You need a tray adapter and possibly terminal modifications. See our [cross-reference tool](../cross-reference/index.md) for compatibility.

### What's the difference between 55D23 and 65D26?
65D26 is longer (260mm vs 232mm) with higher capacity and CCA. It fits larger engine bays. Always check your battery tray dimensions.

### Should I buy a maintenance-free battery?
Maintenance-free (sealed) batteries are convenient but may have shorter life in hot climates because you cannot top up water. In hot regions, some buyers prefer maintainable (flooded) batteries that can be serviced.

### How long does a new battery last?
3–5 years in temperate climates. 2–3 years in hot climates. AGM batteries last 1–2 years longer. Use our [Life Estimator](../life-estimator/index.md) for a personalized estimate.
