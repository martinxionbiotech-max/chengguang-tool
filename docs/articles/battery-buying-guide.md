# Car Battery Buying Guide 2026

## The 5-Step Selection Process

```
Step 1: Standard  →  Is your vehicle JIS, DIN, or BCI?
Step 2: Size      →  Which model/group fits your tray?
Step 3: CCA       →  How much cranking power for your engine + climate?
Step 4: Technology →  SLI, EFB, AGM, or Heavy Duty?
Step 5: Source     →  Retail, wholesale, or OEM private label?
```

## Step 1: Determine Your Battery Standard

Your vehicle is engineered for one standard. Installing the wrong standard means the battery won't physically fit or connect.

| Standard | Terminal | Hold-Down | Case Width Range | Regions |
|----------|----------|-----------|:---:|---------|
| **JIS** | T1 flat tab (small/large) | B00 top frame | 129–278 mm | Asia, Africa, ME, Oceania, S. America |
| **DIN** | Round post | B13 bottom rail | 175 mm (standard) | Europe, N/W Africa, Middle East |
| **BCI** | SAE post or side terminal | Group-dependent | Varies by Group | North America |
| **AS** | T1 (JIS-style) | B00 top frame | Same as JIS | Australia, NZ |
| **SA** | T1 (JIS-style) | B00 top frame | Same as JIS | South Africa |

### Visual Identification

| Terminal Type | Appearance | Cable Connection |
|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| **T1 Small** (JIS) | Flat metal tab, ~12.5mm wide, hole in center | Flat connector bolted through hole |
| **T1 Large** (JIS) | Flat metal tab, ~14.5mm wide, hole in center | Flat connector bolted through hole |
| **SAE/DIN Post** | Round lead post, ~17.5mm (neg) / 19.5mm (pos) | Clamp wraps around post |

!!! danger "Never Assume Interchangeability"
    JIS and DIN batteries look similar from above but are architecturally different. Key incompatibilities: terminal type, hold-down mechanism, case height, and sometimes polarity orientation. Installing a DIN battery in a JIS vehicle (or vice versa) requires an adapter kit and terminal modification. Always check your owner's manual and measure your battery tray.

## Step 3: Select the Right CCA

| Engine | Displacement | Temperate | Cold (−15°C) | Very Cold (−25°C) |
|--------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 4-cyl Gas | 1.6L | 350 | 450 | 550 |
| 4-cyl Gas | 2.0L | 400 | 520 | 640 |
| 4-cyl Gas | 2.5L | 450 | 590 | 720 |
| V6 Gas | 3.5L | 580 | 750 | 930 |
| V8 Gas | 5.0L | 750 | 980 | 1,200 |
| 4-cyl Diesel | 2.5L | 650 | 850 | 1,040 |
| V6 Diesel | 3.0L | 850 | 1,100 | 1,360 |

> **[Calculate your exact CCA: CCA Calculator →](../cca-calculator/index.md)**

### The 20% Margin Rule

Always add **20% margin** above your calculated minimum CCA. Why:
- CCA degrades 5–10% per year — a 20% margin gives you 2–4 years of comfortable starting
- Cold snaps can be 5–10°C colder than "normal" winter temperatures
- Aging oil and starter motors draw more current than new ones

## Step 4: Choose Battery Technology

| Technology | Cost | Life (Temperate) | Life (Hot) | Best Application |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|
| **SLI (Flooded)** | $ (1×) | 4–5 yr | 2–3 yr | Standard vehicles, budget |
| **EFB** | $$ (1.3×) | 4–5 yr | 2.5–3.5 yr | Entry start-stop, hot climates |
| **AGM** | $$$ (1.5–2×) | 5–7 yr | 2.5–4 yr | Start-stop, luxury, extreme climates |
| **Heavy Duty** | $$$$ (2–3×) | 4–5 yr | 2–3 yr | Trucks, buses, equipment |

### Decision Matrix

| Your Situation | Best Choice |
|----------------|------------|
| Budget, temperate climate, standard vehicle | SLI |
| Budget, hot climate, want longer life | EFB or AGM |
| Start-stop vehicle (any climate) | AGM |
| Luxury vehicle with high electrical load | AGM |
| Cold climate (−20°C or below) | AGM |
| Fleet/commercial trucks | Heavy Duty |
| Want maintenance-free (never add water) | AGM |

### Total Cost of Ownership Comparison (5-Year)

| Technology | Upfront Cost | Replacement Cycle | 5-Year Cost |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| SLI | $100 | Replace at 3 yr + 5 yr | **$200** |
| EFB | $130 | Replace at 4 yr | **$130** |
| AGM | $180 | Lasts full 5 yr | **$180** |

*In hot climates, AGM may actually be cheaper over 5 years despite higher upfront cost because it avoids a mid-cycle replacement.*

### How do I know what battery fits my car?
Check your owner's manual, read your current battery label, or use our [Battery Finder](../finder/index.md). Always measure your tray before purchasing.

### What happens if I buy the wrong standard?
JIS→DIN mismatch requires tray adapter + terminal adapter + hood clearance verification. Buy the correct standard.

### Is a more expensive battery always better?
No. Match technology to needs. AGM is worth it for start-stop, luxury, or extreme climates. Standard SLI is fine for temperate climate daily drivers.

### Can I install a car battery myself?
Yes, with basic tools. Disconnect (−) first, connect (+) first. Wear gloves and eye protection. Some modern vehicles need a memory saver.

### What is the warranty on a car battery?
SLI: 12–24 mo free replacement. AGM: 24–36 mo. Hot climate warranties are shorter. Register and keep receipt.
